I'm fascinated by the human brain, and that fascination sits at the core of how I design. What motivates behaviour is deeply ingrained and multifaceted.

Nine years in e-commerce digital marketing, spanning subscription, DTC, own brands, healthtech, fashion and beauty, sparked my desire to know customers more deeply. That curiosity led me into UX. Since 2021 I've been a Product Designer at HelloFresh.

With foundational knowledge of user patterns across these industries, I bring a holistic perspective to every project. By understanding user needs and the broader ecosystem a product lives in, the problem becomes clear.

When I'm not designing, you'll find me hiking in the mountains, digging for vintage treasures, or picking up knitting stitches on the couch.
